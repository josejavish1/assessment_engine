### 1. MACROECONOMIC CALIBRATION FUNCTIONS (THE 4 UNIVERSAL SCALARS)

To prevent model overfitting to any specific tenant while eliminating the dual pathologies of **Statutory Ceiling Inflation** (assuming maximum legal fines and 100% cloud/revenue exposure) and **Monolithic Downtime Myopia** (ignoring non-outage losses), the engine introduces four dimensionless macroeconomic damping scalars: $\Omega$, $\beta$, $\gamma$, and $\theta$. 

These scalars are derived strictly from the tenant's **Operational Archetype**, **IT Topology**, and **Regulatory Jurisdiction**, completely decoupling the mathematical engine from tenant identity.

#### 1.1. Operational Perimeter & Jurisdictional Exposure Ratio ($\Omega_{\text{perimeter}} \in (0, 1]$)
Enterprise risk assessments rarely encompass 100% of a multinational conglomerate's holding structure, nor do regional regulations (e.g., EU NIS2, Brazil LGPD) apply to extra-territorial holding subsidiaries with non-interconnected IT/OT fabrics.

$$\Omega_{\text{perimeter}} = \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{R_{\text{assessed\_scope}}}{R_{\text{global\_consolidated}}} \cdot \kappa_{\text{coupling}}\right)$$

*   $R_{\text{assessed\_scope}}$: Revenue generated by the business units within the technical boundary of the assessment.
*   $R_{\text{global\_consolidated}}$: Total gross turnover of the parent holding entity.
*   $\kappa_{\text{coupling}} \in [1.0, 1.5]$: Contagion multiplier representing shared Active Directory, ERP, or flat OT network topology across unassessed subsidiaries. If the tenant does not declare scoped revenue, $\Omega_{\text{perimeter}}$ defaults to the archetype's empirical **Jurisdictional Footprint Prior** ($\Omega_0$).

#### 1.2. Revenue Disruption Elasticity & Physical Buffering Factor ($\beta_{\text{buffering}} \in [0.10, 1.00]$)
An hour of IT downtime does not equal an hour of permanently lost gross revenue across all verticals. Physical inventory buffers, asynchronous batch processing, and contractually deferred delivery schedules absorb transient availability shocks.

$$\beta_{\text{buffering}} = 1.0 - \left( \alpha_{\text{inventory}} \cdot w_{\text{ot}} + \alpha_{\text{deferral}} \cdot (1 - w_{\text{ot}}) \right) \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{\text{MTTR}_{\text{expected}}}{\tau_{\text{exhaustion}}}\right)$$

*   $\alpha_{\text{inventory}} \in [0, 0.85]$: Physical stock/silo buffering capacity (e.g., clinker silos in manufacturing vs. zero buffer in instant payments).
*   $\alpha_{\text{deferral}} \in [0, 0.70]$: B2B contractual SLA grace period elasticity before financial penalties or order cancellations trigger.
*   $w_{\text{ot}} \in [0, 1]$: Operational Technology (OT) weight vs. transactional IT weight.
*   $\tau_{\text{exhaustion}}$: Buffer exhaustion threshold (in hours) after which manual workarounds fail and revenue loss becomes linear ($1:1$).

#### 1.3. Infrastructure Topology & Cloud Intensity Scalar ($\gamma_{\text{cloud}} \in [0.05, 0.95]$)
FinOps waste, elastic compute over-provisioning, and shadow SaaS sprawl scale proportionally with OpEx cloud adoption, not gross corporate revenue. Legacy CapEx-heavy, on-premise estates suffer from hardware maintenance lock-in rather than elastic billing runaway.

$$\gamma_{\text{cloud}} = \frac{\text{Spend}_{\text{cloud\_opex}} + \text{Spend}_{\text{saas}}}{\text{Spend}_{\text{total\_it}}} = \gamma_{\text{archetype\_prior}} \cdot \left(1 + \delta_{\text{maturity\_drift}}\right)$$

*   Where $\text{Spend}_{\text{total\_it}} = R_{\text{global}} \cdot \Omega_{\text{perimeter}} \cdot \rho_{\text{it\_intensity}}$, and $\rho_{\text{it\_intensity}} \in [0.012, 0.085]$ is the industry-standard IT spend-to-revenue ratio.

#### 1.4. Supervisory Enforcement Discretion Scalar ($\theta_{\text{regulatory}} \in [0.02, 0.40]$)
Statutory frameworks (GDPR Art. 83, NIS2 Art. 34, DORA, LGPD) define **maximum theoretical ceilings** (e.g., 2% to 4% of global turnover). Empirical actuarial data from European Data Protection Boards (EDPB), ANPD, and SEC enforcement actions prove that actual levied fines follow a log-normal distribution centered far below the statutory cap, heavily modulated by negligence vs. good-faith maturity.

$$\theta_{\text{regulatory}}(M) = \theta_{\text{base\_enforcement}} \cdot \exp\left(-\lambda_{\text{mitigation}} \cdot \left(\frac{M - 1.0}{4.0}\right)\right)$$

*   $\theta_{\text{base\_enforcement}} \in [0.08, 0.35]$: Empirical regulatory aggressiveness of the primary supervisory authority for the given vertical.
*   $M \in [1.0, 5.0]$: Assessed governance and compliance maturity score.
*   $\lambda_{\text{mitigation}} \approx 1.85$: Supervisory leniency decay constant for demonstrable due diligence ("Safe Harbor" effect).

```python
from dataclasses import dataclass
from enum import Enum
import math
from typing import Dict, Optional


class OperationalArchetype(str, Enum):
    CONTINUOUS_DIGITAL_FINTECH = "CONTINUOUS_DIGITAL_FINTECH"
    PROCESS_MANUFACTURING_OT = "PROCESS_MANUFACTURING_OT"
    LIFE_SAFETY_CRITICAL = "LIFE_SAFETY_CRITICAL"
    HIGH_TEMPO_COMMERCIAL = "HIGH_TEMPO_COMMERCIAL"
    DEFERRED_NETWORK_LOGISTICS = "DEFERRED_NETWORK_LOGISTICS"
    STANDARD_ENTERPRISE_B2B = "STANDARD_ENTERPRISE_B2B"


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class ArchetypePriors:
    it_spend_intensity_ratio: float
    default_perimeter_ratio: float
    buffering_elasticity_beta: float
    cloud_intensity_gamma: float
    regulatory_statutory_cap_pct: float
    base_enforcement_discretion_theta: float
    cyber_frequency_lambda_base: float


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class TenantTelemetryOverrides:
    scoped_revenue_ratio: Optional[float] = None
    declared_it_spend_ratio: Optional[float] = None
    declared_cloud_opex_ratio: Optional[float] = None
    custom_buffering_factor: Optional[float] = None


@dataclass(frozen=True)
class CalibratedRiskVector:
    effective_revenue_perimeter: float
    effective_it_budget: float
    ale_availability_downtime: float
    ale_cyber_extortion_breach: float
    ale_regulatory_sanctions: float
    ale_structural_capital_waste: float

    @property
    def total_annualized_loss_expectancy(self) -> float:
        return (
            self.ale_availability_downtime
            + self.ale_cyber_extortion_breach
            + self.ale_regulatory_sanctions
            + self.ale_structural_capital_waste
        )


class UniversalActuarialCalibrationEngine:
    """Deterministic, zero-hardcoding actuarial risk engine.

    Computes calibrated Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) across any industry
    vertical.
    """

    MITIGATION_DECAY_CONSTANT: float = 1.85
    MATURITY_SCALE_MAX: float = 5.0
    MATURITY_SCALE_MIN: float = 1.0

    def __init__(self, archetype_registry: Dict[OperationalArchetype, ArchetypePriors]) -> None:
        self._registry = archetype_registry

    def compute_calibrated_ale(
        self,
        gross_annual_revenue: float,
        archetype: OperationalArchetype,
        maturity_scores: Dict[str, float],
        uncalibrated_base_downtime_loss: float,
        overrides: Optional[TenantTelemetryOverrides] = None,
    ) -> CalibratedRiskVector:
        if gross_annual_revenue <= 0:
            raise ValueError("Gross annual revenue must be strictly positive.")

        priors = self._registry[archetype]
        telemetry = overrides or TenantTelemetryOverrides()

        # 1. Operational Scope & Perimeter Calibration (Omega)
        omega = (
            telemetry.scoped_revenue_ratio
            if telemetry.scoped_revenue_ratio is not None
            else priors.default_perimeter_ratio
        )
        effective_revenue = gross_annual_revenue * max(0.05, min(1.0, omega))

        # 2. IT Spend & Cloud Topology Calibration (Gamma)
        it_intensity = (
            telemetry.declared_it_spend_ratio
            if telemetry.declared_it_spend_ratio is not None
            else priors.it_spend_intensity_ratio
        )
        effective_it_budget = effective_revenue * it_intensity

        gamma = (
            telemetry.declared_cloud_opex_ratio
            if telemetry.declared_cloud_opex_ratio is not None
            else priors.cloud_intensity_gamma
        )

        #Normalized Maturity Deficits: d_i in [0.0, 1.0] where 0 is perfect maturity (5.0)
        m_avail = maturity_scores.get("availability", 2.5)
        m_cyber = maturity_scores.get("cybersecurity", 2.5)
        m_gov = maturity_scores.get("governance", 2.5)
        m_finops = maturity_scores.get("finops_architecture", 2.5)

        deficit_cyber = (self.MATURITY_SCALE_MAX - m_cyber) / 4.0
        deficit_gov = (self.MATURITY_SCALE_MAX - m_gov) / 4.0
        deficit_finops = (self.MATURITY_SCALE_MAX - m_finops) / 4.0

        # 3. Pillar I: Operational Availability & Business Interruption (Beta-damped)
        beta = (
            telemetry.custom_buffering_factor
            if telemetry.custom_buffering_factor is not None
            else priors.buffering_elasticity_beta
        )
        # Applies physical/transactional buffering to raw hourly downtime models
        ale_availability = uncalibrated_base_downtime_loss * beta * omega

        # 4. Pillar II: Cyber Extortion, Incident Response & Forensics
        # Scales with effective IT footprint and cyber maturity deficit
        lambda_cyber = priors.cyber_frequency_lambda_base * (0.4 + 1.2 * deficit_cyber)
        incident_severity_ratio = 0.045 * (0.5 + 0.5 * beta)
        ale_cyber = effective_it_budget * lambda_cyber * incident_severity_ratio * (1.0 + deficit_cyber)

        # 5. Pillar III: Regulatory & Supervisory Sanctions (Theta-damped)
        theta_effective = priors.base_enforcement_discretion_theta * math.exp(
            -self.MITIGATION_DECAY_CONSTANT * ((m_gov - self.MATURITY_SCALE_MIN) / 4.0)
        )
        statutory_ceiling = effective_revenue * priors.regulatory_statutory_cap_pct
        breach_probability_joint = lambda_cyber * 0.15 * deficit_gov
        ale_regulatory = statutory_ceiling * theta_effective * breach_probability_joint

        # 6. Pillar IV: Structural Capital & Cloud FinOps Waste (Gamma-damped)
        # Models stranded CapEx capacity + OpEx cloud elastic waste
        cloud_waste_rate = 0.22 * gamma * deficit_finops
        onprem_inefficiency_rate = 0.065 * (1.0 - gamma) * deficit_finops
        ale_capital_waste = effective_it_budget * (cloud_waste_rate + onprem_inefficiency_rate)

        return CalibratedRiskVector(
            effective_revenue_perimeter=round(effective_revenue, 2),
            effective_it_budget=round(effective_it_budget, 2),
            ale_availability_downtime=round(ale_availability, 2),
            ale_cyber_extortion_breach=round(ale_cyber, 2),
            ale_regulatory_sanctions=round(ale_regulatory, 2),
            ale_structural_capital_waste=round(ale_capital_waste, 2),
        )
```
